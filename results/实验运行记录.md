## Anomalies and decisions

- Initial DADA2 error learning with 1.1e8 requested bases was too slow on Windows and was stopped without using its outputs.
- The 16S filter was revised from `maxEE=2/truncQ=2` to `maxEE=5/truncQ=0` after a parameter check showed the latter retained about 86% rather than 22%–44% of already merged long reads.
- 16S denoising was parallelized into three chunks using one common error model, then merged before a single chimera-removal step.
- ITS taxonomy was rerun twice against the official UNITE v10.0 general FASTA
  release. Only 19/2,481 ASVs differed at one or more terminal ranks. The final
  conservative consensus has 2,481 Fungi, 1,265 genera, and 687 species
  hypotheses; the 19 unstable ASVs are retained in a separate audit table.
- Genus-level differential abundance was completed for both domains on
  unrarefied counts. ALDEx2 used Welch expected BH values (`we.eBH`) with
  `|effect| > 1`; ANCOM-BC2 used BH correction and pseudo-count sensitivity.
  Neither domain produced a two-tool consensus genus.
- ITS retained 262 genera at 10% prevalence and 200 at 25% prevalence. The
  10% analysis produced eight ANCOM-BC2-only candidates; five persisted at 25%.
  ALDEx2 produced no ITS hits at either threshold.
- ANCOM-BC2 reports unstable variance estimates because each group contains only three samples.
- ITS AEFP had the smallest observed distance to the natural centroid, but its
  stratified-bootstrap interval overlapped the other treatments and the exact
  treatment-label permutation was not significant. The claim is retained only
  as a descriptive tendency.

- Natural-reference bootstrap: 20,000 stratified iterations.
- Exact treatment-label permutation: 369,600 assignments.
- Differential abundance: ALDEx2 Welch and ANCOM-BC2 at phylum level,
  10% prevalence, 256 Monte-Carlo instances.
- Result entry: `14_分析可重复性/README.md`.
- Claim-level table: `14_分析可重复性/04_结论汇总/claim_stability_matrix.csv`.
- Endpoint comparison: `14_分析可重复性/04_结论汇总/pipeline_endpoint_matrix.csv`.

Key technical findings:

- Original and current 16S phylum compositions were highly concordant at the
  sample level; ITS was mostly concordant but one As sample changed strongly
  because old `unclassified Fungi` was classified by current UNITE.
- Shannon/Simpson null results were stable, while 16S Observed richness changed
  from non-significant under the original OTU table to significant under ASV.
- Bray-Curtis community separation was strong at ASV level but not at phylum
  level; phylum-level Aitchison remained sensitive to composition but differed
  from Bray-Curtis.
- Adding the unassigned raw group 23 changed the significance of phylum-level
  ITS Bray-Curtis comparisons.
- AEFP was the closest ITS artificial treatment to the natural reference in all
  three available pipelines, but exact label permutation did not confirm the
  ranking. No AEFP advantage was found in 16S.
- ALDEx2 had no phylum-level hits in either domain or pipeline. ANCOM-BC2 had
  a few single-tool candidates whose identities/contrasts changed across old
  and current taxonomies; no dual-tool consensus was found.
